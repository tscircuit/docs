## Overview

In this tutorial, we'll build the schematic for an **Arduino Uno R3** development board using **tscircuit**. Because this is a schematic-focused design (`routingDisabled`), we'll organize the circuit cleanly on the schematic canvas (`schX`, `schY`, `schPortArrangement`) with all essential subsystems:

- **ATmega328P-PU Microcontroller (`U1`)** with complete Arduino pin mapping (`D0`–`D13`, `A0`–`A5`, `RESET`, `XTAL1`/`XTAL2`, `VCC`, `AVCC`, `AREF`, `GND`).
- **16 MHz Clock Oscillator (`Y1`)** with `22pF` load capacitors (`C1`, `C2`) and `1MΩ` feedback resistor (`R1`).
- **5V / 3.3V Power Regulation (`U2` NCP1117-5.0 & `U3` LP2985-3.3)** with input/output bulk and ceramic decoupling capacitors.
- **Reset & Auto-Reset Circuit (`SW1`, `R2` 10kΩ pull-up, `C3` 100nF DTR coupling capacitor)**.
- **Built-in User LED (`D1` on `D13`) & Power Indicator LED (`D2`)**.
- **Standard Arduino Uno Headers (`J1` Power, `J2` Analog, `J3` Digital High, `J4` Digital Low)**.

## Components Overview

| RefDes | Component | Value / Part Number | Role in Circuit |
|--------|-----------|---------------------|-----------------|
| `U1` | Microcontroller | `ATMEGA328P-PU` | Main 8-bit AVR MCU running at 16 MHz |
| `U2` | 5V LDO Regulator | `NCP1117ST50T3G` | Regulates `VIN` (7–12V) down to `+5V` |
| `U3` | 3.3V LDO Regulator | `LP2985-33DBVR` | Regulates `+5V` down to `+3V3` |
| `Y1` | Crystal Resonator | `16MHz` | Primary clock source for `ATmega328P` |
| `C1`, `C2` | Ceramic Capacitors | `22pF` | Crystal load capacitors (`XTAL1`, `XTAL2`) |
| `C3`, `C4`, `C5`, `C6` | Ceramic Capacitors | `100nF` (`0.1uF`) | DTR auto-reset, `VCC`, `AVCC`, and `AREF` decoupling |
| `C7`, `C8` | Electrolytic Capacitors | `47uF` | Bulk filtering on `VIN` and `+5V` rails |
| `R1` | Resistor | `1MΩ` | Parallel feedback across `16MHz` crystal |
| `R2` | Resistor | `10kΩ` | `RESET` pull-up resistor to `+5V` |
| `R3`, `R4` | Resistors | `1kΩ` | Current-limiting resistors for `SCK/D13` LED and `ON` LED |
| `D1`, `D2` | LEDs | Green / Yellow | Built-in `L` (`D13`) LED and `ON` power LED |
| `J1`–`J4` | Pin Headers | 6-pin, 8-pin, 10-pin | Standard Arduino Uno shield headers |

## Key Subsystems Explained

### 1. ATmega328P Pin Organization (`U1`)

Using `schPortArrangement`, we group the 28 pins logically by function rather than physical DIP order:
- **Left Side:** `RESET`, `XTAL1`/`XTAL2` clock inputs, and the 6 analog inputs (`A0`–`A5`).
- **Right Side:** All 14 digital I/O pins (`D0`–`D13`), including hardware UART (`D0`/`D1`), PWM channels, and SPI (`D10`–`D13`).
- **Top Side:** Positive supply rails (`VCC`, `AVCC`) and analog reference (`AREF`).
- **Bottom Side:** Ground pins (`GND1`, `GND2`).

### 2. 16 MHz Clock & Reset Circuit

The ATmega328P uses an external `16MHz` crystal (`Y1`) across `PB6/XTAL1` (`pin9`) and `PB7/XTAL2` (`pin10`) with two `22pF` load capacitors (`C1`, `C2`) to ground and a `1MΩ` parallel feedback resistor (`R1`).

For reliable programming and operation:
- `R2` (`10kΩ`) pulls `PC6/RESET` (`pin1`) up to `+5V`.
- `C3` (`100nF`) couples the `DTR` line from the USB-to-serial bridge to trigger an automatic reset pulse during sketch uploads.

### 3. Dual-Stage Power Regulation (`+5V` and `+3.3V`)

- **`U2` (`NCP1117ST50T3G`):** Steps `VIN` (`7V–12V`) down to `+5V` with `47uF` bulk capacitors (`C7`, `C8`).
- **`U3` (`LP2985-33DBVR`):** Generates the auxiliary `+3.3V` rail from `+5V` for low-voltage sensors and shields.
- **`C4`, `C5`, `C6` (`100nF`):** Placed on `VCC`, `AVCC`, and `AREF` to filter high-frequency switching noise and stabilize ADC readings.
