tscircuit is an open-source electronics design toolchain. Write **TypeScript and
React components** to create schematics, routed printed circuit boards (PCBs),
3D previews, and manufacturing files.

Your circuit is code: reuse a USB-C interface across projects, generate repeated
circuits with a loop, or review a board change in Git. Work in the browser,
in your local editor, or with an [AI assistant](../advanced/ai-context.mdx).

[Try the browser editor](https://tscircuit.com/playground) or [start a local project](./quickstart-cli.md).

## Explore a complete ESP32 design

This [Bluetooth speaker board](https://tscircuit.com/seveibar/esp32-bluetooth-speaker)
combines an ESP32, a MAX98357A I2S audio amplifier, USB-C power and programming,
and a battery power supply on a 62 × 44 mm, two-layer PCB. Its three-sheet
schematic separates USB power, battery power, and the controller and audio circuitry.

## Design an RP2040 motor controller

This [RP2040 motor controller](https://tscircuit.com/imrishabh18/rp2040-motor-controller)
by **imrishabh18** combines a DRV8825 stepper-motor driver, separate USB-C data
and USB-PD power ports, and winding-current and temperature sensing on a
42.30 × 42.32 mm, four-layer PCB. Its TypeScript project brings the controller,
motor driver, power supplies, and sensing circuits into one board design.

## Start building

- **In your browser:** [open the playground](https://tscircuit.com/playground) and explore [community designs](https://tscircuit.com).
- **In your editor:** [create a local project](./quickstart-cli.md) and preview changes with `tsci dev`.
- **With AI:** [install the tscircuit skill](../advanced/ai-context.mdx) for your assistant.

Learn the [essential circuit elements](../guides/tscircuit-essentials/essential-elements.mdx),
[import real parts from JLCPCB](../guides/importing-modules-and-chips/importing-from-jlcpcb.mdx),
and combine [automatic layout](../guides/tscircuit-essentials/automatic-pcb-layout.mdx)
with [manual placement and routing](../guides/tscircuit-essentials/manual-edits.mdx).
