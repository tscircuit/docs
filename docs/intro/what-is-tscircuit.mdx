tscircuit is an open-source electronics design toolchain. Write **TypeScript and
React components** to create schematics, routed printed circuit boards (PCBs),
3D previews, and manufacturing files.

Your circuit is code: reuse a USB-C interface across projects, generate repeated
circuits with a loop, or review a board change in Git. Work in the browser,
in your local editor, or with an [AI assistant](../advanced/ai-context.mdx).

## Simulate a circuit from the same source

Add sources, probes, and a simulation to your circuit to inspect its analog
behavior. This RC filter uses SPICE to show the capacitor charging toward the
5 V input. Open it in the editor and change the resistance or capacitance to
see how the response changes.

<CircuitPreview
  defaultView="schematic"
  hidePCBTab
  hide3DTab
  verticalStack
  showSimulationGraph
  code={`
export default () => (
  <board routingDisabled>
    <voltagesource name="V1" voltage="5V" schX={-4} schY={1} schRotation={270} />
    <resistor name="R1" resistance="1k" schX={-1} />
    <capacitor name="C1" capacitance="1uF" schX={2} schY={1} schRotation={270} />

    <trace from=".V1 > .pin1" to=".R1 > .pin1" />
    <trace from=".R1 > .pin2" to=".C1 > .pin1" />
    <trace from=".C1 > .pin2" to="net.GND" />
    <trace from=".V1 > .pin2" to="net.GND" />

    <voltageprobe name="INPUT" connectsTo=".V1 > .pin1" />
    <voltageprobe name="OUTPUT" connectsTo=".C1 > .pin1" />
    <analogsimulation duration="5ms" timePerStep="0.05ms" spiceEngine="ngspice" />
  </board>
)
`}
/>

Explore more [SPICE simulation examples](../guides/spice-simulation/introduction.mdx).

## Bring electronics and mechanics together

React components can also describe printed parts and mechanical assemblies.
Here, a NEMA17 motor and a PCB mount to named faces on a bracket defined with
JSCAD. The same project contains the bracket geometry, mounting holes, and
board placement.

## Start building

- **In your browser:** [open the playground](https://tscircuit.com/playground) and explore [community designs](https://tscircuit.com).
- **In your editor:** [create a local project](./quickstart-cli.md) and preview changes with `tsci dev`.
- **With AI:** [install the tscircuit skill](../advanced/ai-context.mdx) for your assistant.

Learn the [essential circuit elements](../guides/tscircuit-essentials/essential-elements.mdx),
[import real parts from JLCPCB](../guides/importing-modules-and-chips/importing-from-jlcpcb.mdx),
and combine [automatic layout](../guides/tscircuit-essentials/automatic-pcb-layout.mdx)
with [manual placement and routing](../guides/tscircuit-essentials/manual-edits.mdx).
