A harness drawing is the sheet a cable shop builds from: both connectors, which pin each wire lands on, the cut length, and the parts to order. Describe the cable once with [`assembly.cable`](../elements/assembly-cable.mdx) and [`assembly.cablewire`](../elements/assembly-cablewire.mdx), then place it on an [`assembly.drawing.page`](../elements/assembly-drawing-page.mdx) with [`assembly.drawing.harnessview`](../elements/assembly-drawing-harnessview.mdx).

:::info
Harness drawings need `tscircuit` 0.0.3290 or later.
:::

## Draw a cable

Give the cable a `length` and one `assembly.cablewire` per conductor. `fromPin` and `toPin` are contact positions on the cable's own two connectors.

<CircuitPreview wrapCode code={harnessBasic.code} drawingPages={harnessBasic.pages} defaultView="drawing" hideSchematicTab hidePCBTab hide3DTab />

Both housings are drawn from the same face of the cable, so pin 1 is at the top of the `from` end and at the bottom of the `to` end. A pin-to-pin cable therefore shows its half twist. The drawn length is not to scale; the dimension states the `length` you authored.

## Add a wiring list

[`assembly.drawing.wiringtable`](../elements/assembly-drawing-wiringtable.mdx) lists every wire in a table. This adapter joins a 1 mm pitch JST SH plug to a 2 mm pitch JST PH plug, swapping power and ground on the way:

<CircuitPreview wrapCode code={harnessSignals.code} drawingPages={harnessSignals.pages} defaultView="drawing" hideSchematicTab hidePCBTab hide3DTab />

`fromConnectorName` and `toConnectorName` label the two ends in the view and head the table's pin columns. The `SIGNAL`, `COLOR` and `AWG` columns appear because the wires state a `name`, a `color` and a gauge.

## Add a parts list and balloons

[`assembly.drawing.bomtable`](../elements/assembly-drawing-bomtable.mdx) with `partRef` set to the cable lists what the cable is built from. `showBalloons` then points each item number at the part it names. Per-wire `awg` overrides the cable's `wireAwg`, so the power pair and the data pair order as different wire:

## Build a complete manufacturing sheet

A standard sheet size, a [title block](../elements/assembly-drawing-titleblock.mdx) and [notes](../elements/assembly-drawing-note.mdx) turn the same pieces into a drawing you can send out. With `anchorAlignment="bottom_left"` on the page, every position is measured from the sheet's bottom-left corner:

<CircuitPreview wrapCode code={harnessSheet.code} drawingPages={harnessSheet.pages} defaultView="drawing" hideSchematicTab hidePCBTab hide3DTab />

Save it as `motor-cable.circuit.tsx` and export the sheet by its page name:

```bash
tsci export motor-cable.circuit.tsx --drawing motor-cable
```

## Limits

- Both ends of a wired cable must have the same number of contacts. Use larger housings and leave cavities empty when the ends differ.
- A harness view is a flattened diagram. It does not show the cable's 3D route, and `length` is the authored build length, never measured from the route.
- Wire colors are listed in the wiring list. The view itself is drawn in black line work.
