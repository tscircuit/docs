**Status: 🚧 Not supported yet on this board.** FastHenry fails on the AM3352
`routed-copper` case:

- separate meshing of touching GND bands gives `filaments had identical centers`
  ([#45](https://github.com/tscircuit/pcb-spice-pipeline/pull/45));
- after that, finely sampled curves give `mutual inductance = infinity`
  ([#46](https://github.com/tscircuit/pcb-spice-pipeline/issues/46));
- refined meshes don't converge at 1 GHz.

**Goal:** remove the straightening approximation.

## The idea

`straightened-stages` swaps the route for straight segments over a finite
return bar. `routed-copper` meshes what's actually on the board:

- the curves and 45° bends;
- the whole-board GND planes (`groundMeshSize`);
- the vias (`via: { platingThickness, equivalentWidth, omitThroughViaStubs }`).

## Build

This is the default in `ibis.circuit.tsx` when you pass no `pcbSpiceChannel`:

```tsx
<DdrIbisBoard circuitJson={physicalRecords} lanes={["DQ"]} activities={["QUIET"]} />
```

## Why it's hard

DQ0 sits 0.12 mm above `inner1`, so the ground cells under it have to be about
0.1 mm. At that size, the three whole-board planes are far too large to mesh.

## Check by hand

Once it runs, compare it with lesson 16. On a solid plane, curved vs straight
loop L differed by about 4 % (9.53 nH vs 9.89 nH). A much larger difference
points to a modelling problem, not physics.
