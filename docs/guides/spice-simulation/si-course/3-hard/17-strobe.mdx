**Status: 🚧 Not supported yet.** On this board, DQS0/DQSn0 change layer onto
`inner2`, between the `inner1` and `inner3` planes. `straightened-stages`
can't share stages between DQ and DQS yet
([pcb-spice-pipeline#49](https://github.com/tscircuit/pcb-spice-pipeline/issues/49)),
and the via stages still need a hand-written `via` profile.

**Goal:** check DQ against its own strobe, not an ideal clock.

## The idea

DDR3 has no shared data clock. Each byte has a differential strobe, DQS/DQSn.

- **Writes:** the controller places the DQS edge in the **centre** of the DQ
  eye, half a UI later.
- **Reads:** the RAM sends DQS **edge-aligned**, and the controller's PHY
  shifts it internally.

## Build

The repo already declares this in `simulations-next/run-ddr.circuit.tsx`:

```tsx
<DdrIbisBoard circuitJson={physicalRecords}
  lanes={["DQ", "DQS", "DQSn"]} activities={["QUIET", "SWITCHING"]} />
```

That adds:

- DQS and DQSn stimuli starting at `uiNs / 2`, with DQSn `inverted`;
- a differential probe, `<voltageprobe name="DQS_RX" connectsTo={dqs.load}
  referenceTo={dqsn.load} />`;
- two experiments, **QUIET** (the strobe parked) and **SWITCHING** (the strobe
  toggling every UI);
- a second eye on `DQS_RX`.

## What you should see

- A DQS differential eye centred on the DQ eye.
- A **switching − quiet** ΔV table in the report. That's how much the toggling
  strobe disturbs DQ through coupling.

## Check by hand

The DQS crossing should sit about 0.78 ns (UI/2) after the DQ transitions. A
skew larger than about a quarter UI eats directly into setup or hold.
