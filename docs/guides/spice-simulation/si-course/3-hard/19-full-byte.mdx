**Status: 🚧 Not supported yet.** `pcbSpiceChannel.lanes` takes at most
three lanes, and shared DQ/DQS stages are blocked by
[pcb-spice-pipeline#49](https://github.com/tscircuit/pcb-spice-pipeline/issues/49).

**Goal:** find the worst bit in the byte, with its neighbours switching.

## The idea

Bits in a byte are routed close together. A victim's worst case is when its
neighbours switch the opposite way. Length mismatch inside the byte also skews
bits against DQS.

## Target setup

- **Lanes:** `DQ0` … `DQ7`, `DM0`, `DQS0` and `DQSn0`, each resolved with
  `resolveDdrChannel`.
- **Stimuli:** one PRBS per bit, with a different seed or a shifted start for
  each, so the neighbours aren't in lockstep.
- **Eyes:** one per DQ, all against the DQS crossing.

## What you should see

Ten eyes. The one in the middle of the bus, with the most coupled neighbours
and the longest route, is usually the worst. Report the worst bit, not the
average.

## Check by hand

- Skew budget: the length difference to DQS × about 6–7 ps/mm should stay
  well inside the setup/hold window from lesson 21.
- Coupling: rerun lesson 15's spacing rule on the tightest pair in the byte.
