**Status: ✅ Runs today** (about 2 minutes per run)

**Goal:** learn what an IBIS model gives you, and why drive strength can be
wrong in either direction.

## What an IBIS file is

A chip vendor publishes an `.ibs` file per part. It holds:

- I–V tables for the pull-up, the pull-down and the clamps;
- V–t waveforms for rising and falling edges into test loads;
- pin capacitance (`C_comp`);
- package R, L and C.

It describes the pin's behaviour without giving away the transistors. A
`[Model Selector]` lists the settings you can choose, such as drive strength
or ODT.

`<ibismodel>` binds that file to a chip. You have to state:

| Prop | Why |
| --- | --- |
| `ibisPinNameToModelName` | Which buffer each pin uses, from the selector |
| `ibisPinNameToReferencePortLabels` | Which pins are the pull-up, pull-down and clamp rails. Nothing is guessed. |
| `modelValueCorner` | `typical`, `minimum` (slow, weak) or `maximum` (fast, strong) |
| `includePackageParasitics` | Whether the package R, L and C are included |

`ibisPinStates` on the experiment sets each pin as a driver or a receiver,
**per experiment**.

## What you see

At 2 ns per bit:

| Drive | Output R | RX swing | Eye height | Eye width |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4 mA | 75 Ω, 20 mA limit | 0.04 → 3.30 V | **0.17 V** | **0.59 ns** |
| 12 mA | 30 Ω | −0.35 → 3.66 V | **3.30 V** | **1.99 ns** |
| 24 mA | 15 Ω | −0.88 → 4.20 V | 3.29 V | 2.0 ns |

## Check by hand

- **Too weak (4 mA):** the current limit sets the first step:
  20 mA × 45.6 Ω ≈ **0.9 V**. The line then charges in steps, one per
  1.06 ns round trip, and doesn't reach the 1.65 V threshold early enough.
  The eye collapses even though the edge rate is slow.
- **About right (12 mA):** 30 Ω against 45.6 Ω gives Γ = −0.21 at the source.
  The far end overshoots once, to 3.66 V, and the next reflection is small.
- **Too strong (24 mA):** 15 Ω gives Γ = −0.50, so the line rings hard
  (−0.88 V to 4.20 V). The eye still passes at 2 ns, but it fails at 0.8 ns
  (lesson 11).

Pick the drive whose output impedance is closest to Z0, not the strongest
one.

## Try this

Set `corner="minimum"` on the 12 mA board. The weak corner adds 30 % to the
drive resistance. Note how much eye height you lose. You'll use that spread
