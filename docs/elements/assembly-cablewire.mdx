`assembly.cablewire` is one insulated conductor of its parent [`assembly.cable`](./assembly-cable.mdx). It says which contact the wire leaves at the cable's `from` end and which contact it lands on at the `to` end. Wires feed [harness drawings](../guides/wire-harness-drawings.mdx) and the cable's parts list; they do not create electrical traces.

## Properties

| Property | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| `fromPin` | `number \| string` | Required contact position on the connector at the cable's `from` end: `1` or `"pin1"`. |
| `toPin` | `number \| string` | Required contact position on the connector at the cable's `to` end. |
| `name` | `string` | Signal or conductor name, such as `"SDA"`. Shown in the wiring list's `SIGNAL` column. |
| `color` | `string` | Insulation color as it should read on the drawing, such as `"black"` or `"red/white"`. |
| `awg` | `number \| string` | Conductor gauge: `22` or `"22awg"`. Omit to inherit the cable's `wireAwg`. |

Pins are positions on the cable's plugs, counted from 1. They are not the pin labels of the board connectors the cable mates with.

## Rules

- Each contact takes at most one wire at each end.
- A pin number cannot exceed the connector's contact count.
- A cavity with no wire is left empty.
- A cable with numbered contacts needs at least one wire before it can appear in a harness view or a wiring list.
