`assembly.part` is a generic mechanical component, such as a bearing, bracket, or cover. Give it a stable `name` and optionally supply a CAD model. It has no PCB footprint, schematic symbol, or electrical ports.

## Mount a bearing model

This example seats an 8 × 22 × 7 mm bearing in a printed housing. `mountedTo=".HOUSING > .bearingSeat"` selects the pocket floor; `mountFace="base"` selects the bearing's authored bottom reference. The bearing's origin is at that bottom face, so no CAD offset is needed.

The housing, bearing races and recessed shields are modeled in `models.tsx`; the bolt uses an existing JSCAD Electronics model. [`assembly.referencesurface`](./assembly-referencesurface.mdx) adds attachment frames to these parts without adding material. The bolt uses its own [`fastenedTo`](./assembly-bolt.mdx) prop.

For a purchased bearing CAD file, replace `cadModel={bearingModel}` with `modelUrl="./bearing.glb"` and keep the references at the asset's physical mounting locations. This example's authored model has its bottom at local Z = 0.

## Part properties

| Property | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| `name` | Required stable identity. Trimmed and must not be empty. |
| `displayName` | Optional human-facing name. |
| `model` | Modelprinter/footprinter specification or an HTTP(S) model URL. |
| `modelUrl` | Imported model URL or asset path. |
| `cadModel` | Existing CAD formats and model-alignment transforms, including CAD JSX; see [`cadmodel`](./cadmodel.mdx). |
| `mountedTo` | Target face selector, such as `.HOUSING > .bearingSeat`, or compact path `HOUSING.bearingSeat`. |
| `mountFace` | This part's named mating face. Supply with `mountedTo`. |
| `mountOffset` | Signed distances along the target face's local X/Y tangents: `{ x?, y? }`. Omitted axes are zero. |
| `mountGap` | Nonnegative clearance along the target's outward normal, default zero. |

Supply at most one of `model`, `modelUrl`, or `cadModel`. Omit all three for a part with no geometry. `cadModel={null}` also keeps the identity without rendering a model; it cannot be combined with another model source. For a `jscad` JSX prop, use [`assembly.printedpart`](./assembly-printedpart.mdx).

## Placement and CAD alignment

Standalone unmounted parts use the world origin. Nested parts inherit the nearest assembly's full placement, including tilt. A face mount sets the complete position and orientation; its children and reference surfaces follow that frame. Use `mountOffset` for in-plane adjustment and `mountGap` for separation. Both require `mountedTo`.

`cadModel.positionOffset` and `rotationOffset` align the mesh within that frame. They do not move the assembly origin, its children, or its reference surfaces. An imported model needs authored reference surfaces; core does not infer mounting faces from its mesh.

Coordinates are in millimetres in a right-handed frame: +X right, +Y top, and +Z above. Target selectors resolve within the nearest `assembly.device`, including targets declared later. A selector must match exactly one face; missing or ambiguous references and mounting cycles report errors.

An [`assembly.screen`](./assembly-screen.mdx) can target the part's identity with `connectsTo=".BEARING"`; this uses its assembly origin and creates no electrical connections. Use [`assembly.subassembly`](./assembly-subassembly.mdx) to move a group through one mounting constraint.
